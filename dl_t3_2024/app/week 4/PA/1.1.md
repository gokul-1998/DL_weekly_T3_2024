what is the difference between - log2(8) - loge(8) - log10(8)

- log2(8) = 3
- loge(8) = ln(8) = 2.0794
- log10(8) = 0.9031

- this means that log2(8) is the number that 2 must be raised to in order to get 8
- loge(8) is the number that e must be raised to in order to get 8
- log10(8) is the number that 10 must be raised to in order to get 8

hence log is opposite of exponentiation

![alt text](image-17.png)
- hence we can say that for a given x,
- log2(x) > loge(x) > log10(x) 
-  e=2.71828

- how to find the slope of these lines?
    ```
    - slope of log2(x) = 1/xln(2)
    - slope of loge(x) = 1/x
    - slope of log10(x) = 1/xln(10)
    ```

- in general, slope of `logb(x) = 1/xln(b)`
